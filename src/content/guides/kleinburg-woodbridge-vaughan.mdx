Vaughan's two oldest neighbourhoods make a good pair for a Saturday. Kleinburg has the galleries and a heritage streetscape; Woodbridge has the Humber valley and Italian bakeries that have been at it for decades. Keep it under $20 by choosing one paid stop.

<Stop slug="woodbridge-farmers-market">

Start in Woodbridge on a Saturday morning between May and October. The market has local produce and Italian specialty foods, and it is free to walk through.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="italian-bakery-row-woodbridge">

Then breakfast on Woodbridge Avenue: espresso for $2 to $3.50 and a pastry for $2 to $5. Sfogliatelle, cornetti, and proper cannoli, made by people who have done it their whole lives.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="boyd-conservation-area">

Walk it off along the Humber River at Boyd, a free conservation area with trails through woodland and open meadow, open from dawn to dusk.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="kortright-centre-for-conservation">

If you want more trail, Kortright has 16 km of it through forest, wetland, and meadow, for $10 for adults. This is the one paid stop; skip it and the day stays nearly free.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="kleinburg-village">

In the afternoon, drive up to Kleinburg, one of the few genuinely historic streetscapes in York Region.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="kleinburg-village-art-walk">

Its small main street holds a surprising number of independent galleries and public art pieces, all walkable and most free to enter.

</Stop>
