This is a day for the north and west of Vaughan, with a village, a conservation centre, and a market. You will want a car or a transit plan, since the stops are spread out.

<Stop slug="woodbridge-farmers-market">

Saturday mornings from June to October, 9am to 1pm. Browsing is free, and it is the easiest place to buy lunch for later.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="kortright-centre-for-conservation">

Trails and nature programs in the Humber Valley, on Pine Valley Drive. There is an admission fee, so check the current price before you go.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="kleinburg-village">

Finish on Islington Avenue with a walk through the village, which has shops and places to eat along the main street.

</Stop>

## Before you go

The McMichael Canadian Art Collection in Kleinburg is closed for a rebuild and plans to reopen in 2029, so do not plan around it.
