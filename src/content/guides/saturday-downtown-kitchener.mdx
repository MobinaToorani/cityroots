A Saturday in downtown Kitchener works best on foot. Everything here is close together, and you need money only for lunch.

<Stop slug="kitchener-market">

A fire damaged the market building in July 2026. The Saturday farmers' market continues outdoors from 7am to 2pm, and buying from it helps the vendors. Check the market's site for the latest news first.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="kw-art-gallery-kwag">

The region's public art gallery, inside Centre in the Square. It is closed on Mondays and opens on Saturdays, so it suits this day well.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="kitchener-public-library-central-branch">

A few minutes up Queen Street. It is a good warm place to sit and read, with no need to spend anything.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="victoria-park">

End with a walk around the lake in the City's best-known park. It is free and open all year.

</Stop>

## Before you go

From December 3 to 13, 2026, the Christkindl Market is on, which makes downtown busier and is worth planning around.
