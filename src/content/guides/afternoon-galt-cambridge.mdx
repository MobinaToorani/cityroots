The old Galt core of Cambridge sits on the Grand River, and it has a market, a free gallery, and a riverside walk within a small area.

<Stop slug="cambridge-farmers-market">

Start at the market on Dickson Street. Check the City's page for its current days, and plan to buy lunch here if it is open.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="cambridge-art-galleries">

Free public galleries beside the river at Queen's Square. See what is showing before you go.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="cambridge-centre-for-the-arts">

The City's arts centre, a short walk from the market. The gallery is free, and it is worth a look if a class or a show is on.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="riverside-park">

For a longer day, drive up to this large City park. It is free and open all year.

</Stop>

## Before you go

Hours vary at each stop, so tap through and check them on the day.
