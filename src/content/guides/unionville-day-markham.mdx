Unionville's Main Street is easy to walk, and the gallery on it is free. The street is being restored, so check for construction before you come.

<Stop slug="unionville-main-street">

Start at the historic street itself. Shops and cafes are open while the work goes on, though some stretches may be fenced off.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="varley-art-gallery-of-markham">

The City's public art gallery, right on Main Street, with free admission. It is closed on Mondays, and weekday hours are short, from noon to 4pm.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="toogood-pond-park">

A short walk away, and the place to sit down afterward. It is free and open all year.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="unionville-stiver-mill-farmers-market">

If you are here on a Sunday from June to October, the market runs from 9am to 2pm. Plan lunch around it.

</Stop>

## Before you go

Budget for lunch and not much else. The Main Street restoration may also change where you can park.
