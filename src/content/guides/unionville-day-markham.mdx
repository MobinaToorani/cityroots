Unionville is a preserved 19th-century main street inside Markham, and it is easy to spend a whole day there for the price of lunch. Finish across the city in Milliken, where Hong Kong style cafes serve a full meal for less than a downtown brunch.

<Stop slug="unionville-farmers-market">

On Saturdays from June through October, start at the market on Main Street: local produce, baked goods, preserves, and honey from York Region farms. It is free to browse.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="unionville-main-street">

Walk the length of Main Street. The heritage storefronts and independent shops are worth seeing even if you buy nothing, and the street leads straight down to the water.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="toogood-pond-park">

Follow the paved trail around Toogood Pond, a historic millpond with green space and benches. In winter, when conditions allow, people skate on it.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="varley-art-gallery-of-markham">

Back on Main Street, the Varley is a free public gallery devoted to Frederick Varley of the Group of Seven, alongside contemporary Canadian art. It is open Tuesday to Sunday.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="unionville-mural-and-public-art-walk">

On your way out, check the laneways and building walls for the village's collection of murals and public art.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="hong-kong-style-cafe-row">

Then drive or take transit to Milliken for a late lunch at a cha chaan teng: milk tea for $3 to $5, a full meal for $10 to $16. Condensed milk toast is the classic order.

</Stop>
