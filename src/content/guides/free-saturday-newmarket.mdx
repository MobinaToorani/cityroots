Downtown Newmarket is small enough to do on foot, and none of this costs anything. From May to October the day starts at the market. The rest of the year, start at the museum.

<Stop slug="newmarket-farmers-market">

The market runs on Saturday mornings in season, from 8am to 1pm. Browsing is free, and it is the liveliest place downtown while it is on.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="riverwalk-commons">

The Town's public square, right beside the market. It is a good place to sit with whatever you bought, and it hosts community events through the year.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="elman-w-campbell-museum">

The Town's local history museum on Main Street, free to visit. It closes for an hour at noon, and it is closed on Sundays and Mondays.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="newmarket-public-library">

A free place to rest and read, and its calendar often has something on. See what is running this week before you walk over.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="fairy-lake-park">

End the day outside. It is a short walk from downtown and free all year.

</Stop>

## Before you go

The market dates change each year, so check them if you are going near the start or end of the season.
