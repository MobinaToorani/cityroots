A warm Saturday in Newmarket can be spent entirely on foot, entirely downtown, and without spending anything. This route stays within a short walk of Main Street and the East Holland River.

<Stop slug="newmarket-farmers-market">

The Saturday market runs through the warmer months at Market Square. It is free to walk through, and vendors take cash and card if you decide breakfast is worth a few dollars after all.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="main-street-historic-district">

From the market, walk Main Street itself. It is one of the most walkable heritage main streets in Ontario, and browsing the independent shops costs nothing.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="main-street-mural-trail">

Look down the laneways as you go. The town has commissioned large murals along Main Street and the streets behind it, celebrating local history and the natural spaces around Newmarket.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="riverwalk-commons-river-path">

Drop down to Riverwalk Commons and follow the paved path along the river. It is flat, short, and a good reset before the afternoon.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="newmarket-public-library">

The library is open until 6pm on Saturdays, right in the heart of downtown. Sit somewhere quiet, or check the program calendar for a book club or a digital help session.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="fairy-lake-park">

End the day at Fairy Lake, a glacial kettle lake surrounded by trails, picnic areas, and wooded paths. In summer the park hosts free outdoor yoga and a community drum circle; both are listed on CityRoots.

</Stop>
