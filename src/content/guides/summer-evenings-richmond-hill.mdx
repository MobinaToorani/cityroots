Richmond Hill does its best free programming in July and August, and most of it happens after work. Here is a week of summer evenings that never asks for a ticket.

<Stop slug="concerts-in-the-park">

Thursday is the anchor. The city's outdoor concert series plays every Thursday evening through July and August. Bring something to sit on.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="mill-pond-park">

The concerts are at Mill Pond, so arrive early and walk the loop around the pond first. It is the city's most loved park for a reason: trails, benches, and water, all a short walk from Yonge Street.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="movies-under-the-stars">

On select summer Fridays, Richmond Green screens family films outdoors at dusk, with community food vendors on site. The film is free; the snacks are up to you.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="lake-wilcox-park">

For an evening with no schedule at all, head north to Lake Wilcox in Oak Ridges. There are sandy beaches, walking trails on Oak Ridges Moraine terrain, and long summer light over the water.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="free-tennis-courts-richmond-hill-parks">

If you would rather move than sit, the city keeps free public tennis courts in parks across Richmond Hill. There is no booking; courts are first come, first served until dusk.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="open-mic-night-local-venues">

Later in the week, local cafes and bars host free open mic nights, usually on Thursday or Friday evenings, for musicians and spoken word artists who are just getting started.

</Stop>
