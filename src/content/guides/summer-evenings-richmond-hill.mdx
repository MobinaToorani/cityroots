Richmond Hill runs free outdoor events in the summer, and its two best parks are free every day. The Town sets the schedule each year, so look at this year's dates first.

<Stop slug="concerts-in-the-park">

The Town's free summer concert series. Bring a chair or a blanket, and check the Town's page for the current lineup.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="moonlight-movies">

Free outdoor movies from the Town. Dates and locations are posted on its site each summer.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="mill-pond-park">

Worth a walk on any evening, with or without an event. It is one of the Town's best-known parks.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="lake-wilcox-park">

A long summer evening by the water. Non-residents should check the parking rules, which apply from April to September.

</Stop>

## Before you go

These are summer events. The rest of the year, the two parks are still open and the city page lists what is on now.
