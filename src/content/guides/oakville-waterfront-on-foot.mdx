Oakville's best asset is free and public: the Lake Ontario shoreline. This walk starts downtown and ends at one of the Town's lakefront parks.

<Stop slug="oakville-museum">

Start at the Town's museum, near the harbour. Admission is by donation, and it opens at 1pm from Tuesday to Sunday.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="oakville-waterfront-trail">

Follow the waterfront trail from the downtown lakefront. Go as far as you like and turn back whenever you want.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="oakville-galleries">

A public contemporary art gallery on Lakeshore Road East. Check what is showing before you make the trip.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="coronation-park">

A large lakefront park and an easy place to stop, free all year.

</Stop>

## Before you go

The lake is windy at any time of year, so bring one more layer than you think you need. Bronte Harbour is further west if you want a longer day.
