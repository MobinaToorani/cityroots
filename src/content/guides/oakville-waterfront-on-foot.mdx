Oakville's best asset is free and public: the Lake Ontario shoreline. This walk follows the waterfront from downtown west to Bronte, with a gallery stop and a ravine detour along the way.

<Stop slug="oakville-waterfront-trail">

Begin downtown on the paved multi-use trail that runs along the lake through downtown and on toward Bronte Harbour. Go as far as your legs want; every stop below sits on or near it.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="oakville-galleries">

Oakville Galleries shows contemporary Canadian and international art in two free spaces, one in the historic Gairloch Gardens estate on the water. It is open Wednesday to Sunday from noon.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="sixteen-mile-creek-trail">

For a change of scenery, the Sixteen Mile Creek trail follows the creek through forested ravines in central Oakville. Watch the bridge underpasses for murals and commissioned art.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="kerr-village">

Heading west, Kerr Village is Oakville's independent main street: locally owned shops and restaurants, free to wander.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="coronation-park">

Coronation Park has mature trees, picnic areas, a beach, and paths at the water's edge. It is a good place to stop for whatever you packed.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="bronte-harbour-sunrise-and-golden-hour">

Finish at Bronte Harbour around golden hour: sailboats, a lighthouse, a boardwalk, and open views across the lake.

</Stop>
