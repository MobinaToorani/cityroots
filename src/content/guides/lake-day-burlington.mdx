Burlington's downtown runs right to Lake Ontario, and you can spend a full day here without paying for anything.

<Stop slug="burlington-waterfront-trail">

Start at Spencer Smith Park and walk the lakefront. The trail is open all year.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="art-gallery-of-burlington">

A public gallery on Lakeshore Road, a short walk from the park. It is the indoor part of the day, and a good reason to come when the wind picks up.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="burlington-public-art-collection">

The City keeps a list of its public art. Use it to choose a few pieces on your way back through downtown.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="royal-botanical-gardens-hiking-trails">

If you have the energy, the trails are a short drive west along Plains Road. Check the site for what is free in the current season, since parts of the gardens charge admission.

</Stop>

## Before you go

Check the city page for what is on this week. The library's free programs are listed there too.
