Toronto is expensive, but its downtown has a full day of good places that cost nothing to enter. Transit links all of these stops.

<Stop slug="st-lawrence-market">

Go early on a Saturday, when it is busiest. Browsing is free, so spend only if something tempts you.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="allan-gardens-conservatory">

City-run greenhouses with free admission, and the warmest place downtown in winter. Check the City's page for current hours.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="toronto-reference-library">

Open from 9am to 5pm on Saturdays. Its atrium is worth seeing, and you can sit as long as you like.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="kensington-market">

End with a walk through the market's streets, where looking is free and food is cheap.

</Stop>
