Rain in Uptown Waterloo is not a reason to stay home. The best of it is indoors and close together, and the only money you need is whatever you spend on lunch.

<Stop slug="vincenzos">

Start with lunch from this Italian grocery and deli on Caroline Street. Check its Sunday hours first, or bring something from home.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="canadian-clay-and-glass-gallery">

A few minutes down the street. This public gallery shows contemporary ceramics and glass, and admission is free. Doors open at 1pm on Sundays, and an hour is about right.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="waterloo-public-library">

A comfortable place to spend the wet part of the afternoon, with reading space and no expectation that you buy anything. Sunday hours are shorter, so check them first.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="waterloo-park">

If the rain lets up before dark, walk over to the park. It is a few minutes from King Street and free all year.

</Stop>

## Before you go

The gallery is closed on Mondays and opens at 1pm on Sundays. Tap any stop to see its details and its own website.
