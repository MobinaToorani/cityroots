Rain in Uptown Waterloo is not a reason to stay home. Most of what makes the neighbourhood good is indoors and within a few blocks of King Street, and the whole day costs about the price of a coffee and one gallery ticket.

<Stop slug="smile-tiger-coffee">

Start here when it opens on Sunday morning. It is a specialty roaster, so the espresso is the point, and the room fills with students and locals who are in no rush either. A drink is the only money you need to spend before lunch.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="canadian-clay-and-glass-gallery">

One of the only public galleries in Canada devoted entirely to contemporary ceramics and glass. It is open Sundays, adult admission is around $5, and some community days are free, so check before you go. Give it an hour; the work rewards slow looking.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="vincenzos">

If the budget has room, pick up lunch at this Italian grocery and deli, an Uptown fixture since 1967. If it does not, the next stop is a fine place to eat something you brought.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="waterloo-public-library">

The library opens at 1pm on Sundays and is a comfortable place to spend a wet afternoon: good reading spaces, a strong digital collection, and no expectation that you buy anything.

</Stop>

<Stop slug="waterloo-park">

If the rain lets up before dark, walk down to Silver Lake. The park is a few minutes from King Street and free all year.

</Stop>

## Before you go

Sunday hours are shorter than weekday hours across Uptown, and the University of Waterloo Art Gallery is closed on Sundays. Each stop above shows its listed hours; tap through to check them on the day.
